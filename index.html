What two hours actually looks like

No standing in a queue behind fifteen other players. Every session follows the same shape so progress is easy to measure.

First 15 minutes

Warm up and movement

Running, mobility and throwing. Shoulders, back and hamstrings get looked after before anyone picks up a bat, which matters more every year past 25.

Next 30 minutes

Skill block

One technical focus for the week, drilled in small groups. Batters work on a shot, bowlers on a length.

Next 45 minutes

Nets

Every player bats and bowls. Coaches move between wickets, and the camera runs for the review later.

Next 20 minutes

Match scenario

Chase 24 off two overs, or bowl at a set field. Pressure practice, because that is where technique breaks.

Last 10 minutes

Review and homework

What worked, what did not, and one thing to work on before the next session. Clips go out on WhatsApp the same evening.

Questions we get asked first

Do you coach children?

No. SS Academy is licensed for adult coaching only, so every batch is 18 and above. We are not able to take junior or school age players, and we do not keep a waiting list for them.

I have not played properly in ten years. Am I too far gone?

No, and you will not be the only one in the net. The Weekend batch is built for exactly this. We rebuild the basics and manage the load so you are not limping into the office the next morning.

Do I need my own kit before joining?

No. Bring sports shoes and a water bottle for the trial and the first few weeks. We keep loaner pads, gloves and helmets at the ground. Once you settle into a batch we will tell you exactly what to buy and what to skip.

What is the coach to player ratio?

Twelve players per net batch with two coaches, and an extra fielding coach on Saturdays. We cap batches rather than adding more players to a session.

Can I join mid season?

Yes. Admissions stay open all year as long as a batch has space. Fees are charged monthly from the date of joining, with no admission or annual charge.

Will this fit around a full time job?

That is what the timetable is built for. The morning batch finishes by 8 am and the evening batch starts at 7 pm. If work takes you out of town, tell us and we will hold your slot rather than charge you for sessions you cannot attend.

Do you help with league and trial selection?

We do. Performance batch players get a season plan built around trial dates, and we share match data and coach references with selectors when asked.

What happens if it rains?

Sessions move to indoor drills and video work, or we add a make up slot the same week. You will get a WhatsApp message by 5 am for morning batches and by 4 pm for evening ones.
